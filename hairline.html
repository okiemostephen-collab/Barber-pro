THE BARBERPRO HAIR GUIDE

Your hairline deserves care.

Keep your grooming routine gentle. A clean finish should never come at the cost of comfort.

Find your next cut

Ask for a natural line-up

Tell your barber you prefer your natural hairline. Avoid asking for an excessively pushed-back edge.

Avoid constant tension

Very tight braids, twists or styles that pull can irritate the scalp. Choose a comfortable style and speak up if it feels too tight.

Keep the scalp comfortable

Cleanse gently and avoid scratching or harsh treatment. Stop using products that cause irritation.

Give your edges a break

Allow time between tight styles and avoid repeatedly applying strong tension to the same areas.

Maintain your cut

Book shape-ups at a comfortable interval and ask your barber to preserve your preferred natural outline.

When to seek advice

If you notice persistent irritation, unusual shedding or scalp discomfort, speak with a qualified health professional.
