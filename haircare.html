BARBERPRO PRESENTS / THE GROOMING EDIT

THE ART OF
LOOKING SHARP.

Your haircut is only the beginning. Discover how to maintain your style, understand your hair, avoid common grooming mistakes and make every visit to the barber count.

EXPLORE THE STYLE LIBRARY
01 / KNOW YOUR HAIR

A great cut deserves great maintenance.

Curly and coily hair can lose moisture quickly. Understanding your hair texture helps you choose suitable products and build a routine that works.

The goal isn't to use every product on the shelf. It's to understand what your hair needs and keep your routine consistent.

LESS GUESSWORK. MORE CONFIDENCE.
02 / THE STYLE LIBRARY

Find your signature look.

Select a hairstyle to reveal what maintaining it involves, what to tell your barber and what mistakes to avoid.

01 / CLEAN & CLASSIC

Low Fade

A subtle fade that keeps the sides neat while leaving room for different top styles.

DISCOVER MAINTENANCE +

Keep the sides tidy with regular barber visits. Moisturise the top according to your hair's needs. Tell your barber how low you want the fade to begin.

Common mistake: Requesting a fade without agreeing on where it should start.

07 / BRAIDS & PROTECTIVE STYLES

Cornrows & Braids

Keep braided styles clean and comfortable while protecting the scalp.

DISCOVER MAINTENANCE +

Clean the scalp gently, dry the braids thoroughly after washing and avoid styles that pull too tightly. Ask your stylist to keep tension comfortable.

Common mistake: Leaving tight braids in when the scalp feels sore or irritated.

02 / TEXTURE & DETAIL

Afro Shape-up

A natural look that celebrates volume, texture and a carefully shaped outline.

DISCOVER MAINTENANCE +

Use a suitable moisturising product and gently detangle when needed. Ask your barber to shape the outline without removing more length than you want.

Common mistake: Brushing aggressively when the hair is dry and tangled.

03 / NATURAL EXPRESSION

Natural Afro

Full natural hair with plenty of room to express your personal style.

DISCOVER MAINTENANCE +

Use a gentle cleansing routine, conditioner and a suitable moisturiser. Detangle gently, preferably when the hair is conditioned.

Common mistake: Assuming more product always means healthier hair.

04 / BOLD & DEFINED

Textured Crop

A short textured top with a clean outline and a modern finish.

DISCOVER MAINTENANCE +

Ask your barber to preserve the texture on top. Use a small amount of styling product if needed, and avoid heavy buildup.

Common mistake: Using too much product and flattening the texture.

05 / SHARP & REFINED

Waves & Line-up

A defined wave pattern with a neat hairline and a clean finish.

DISCOVER MAINTENANCE +

Brush consistently in the direction your waves naturally form. Keep the scalp clean and use a suitable lightweight moisturiser.

Common mistake: Brushing too aggressively or wearing a tight covering that irritates the scalp.

06 / PERSONAL STYLE

Locs & Twists

A versatile style that requires a thoughtful washing and maintenance routine.

DISCOVER MAINTENANCE +

Keep the scalp clean, dry the hair properly after washing and avoid excessive tension during retwisting.

Common mistake: Retwisting too tightly or too frequently.

BARBERPRO INSIDER TIP

Before your next haircut, save a reference photo of the exact style you want. Tell your barber what you like about it: the fade height, top length, hairline or shape. A picture plus a clear explanation can prevent misunderstandings.

03 / YOUR GROOMING ROUTINE

Small habits. Better hair days.

A simple routine is often more useful than a shelf full of products.

01

Cleanse thoughtfully

Wash according to your scalp, activity and hair needs. Avoid using harsh products that leave your hair excessively dry.

02

Moisturise

Use a suitable conditioner or moisturiser. Coily hair often benefits from gentle, consistent moisture care.

03

Protect your style

Avoid unnecessary friction, excessive heat and hairstyles that place too much tension on your scalp.

04 / THE GROOMING SHELF

Know what you're buying.

Different products serve different purposes. Start with what your hair actually needs instead of buying everything.

✦

Shampoo

Helps remove dirt, sweat and product buildup. Choose a gentle option suitable for your scalp.

✦

Conditioner

Helps soften hair, improve manageability and reduce friction while detangling.

✦

Hair moisturiser

Helps reduce dryness and improve the feel of your hair. Choose a formula suited to your texture.

✦

Wave brush

A useful grooming tool for people maintaining waves. Select a brush that feels comfortable on your scalp.

DON'T FALL FOR EVERY PRODUCT CLAIM

Expensive doesn't automatically mean better. Read the ingredients, check the product seal and expiry date, and follow the label. Stop using a product if it causes irritation. Persistent scalp problems deserve professional medical advice.
