THE ESSENTIALS

What to look for

✦

Hair moisturiser

Look for a lightweight moisturiser suited to your hair texture. Apply as directed and avoid using more than needed.

◉

Leave-in conditioner

Can help soften hair and make detangling easier. Choose a formula designed for your hair type.

◌

Shampoo

Use a gentle cleanser and wash as often as your scalp and activity require. Rinse thoroughly.

✧

Conditioner

Helps soften hair after cleansing. Follow the product directions and rinse when required.

〰

Wave cream and styling products

Use modest amounts of products made for your style. Avoid heavy build-up and wash brushes regularly.

＋

Scalp-friendly care

Stop using a product if it irritates your skin. For persistent scalp concerns, speak with a qualified health professional.
